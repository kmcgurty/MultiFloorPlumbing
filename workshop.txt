title=Multi Floor Plumbing [B42]
description=Plumb any number of rain barrels together, giving you water access in a basement.
description=
description=[h2]What?[/h2]
description=This mod slightly changes the logic around plumbing, making it so you can chain rain barrels together. This means a sink on any floor can have access to water.
description=
description=[h2]Why?[/h2]
description=My kitchen is in a basement, and I was disappointed I couldn't get water to my sink. The other mods (LG Plumbing) are either: AI generated, or too OP.
description=
description=[h2]Instructions[/h2]
description=1. Place a rain barrel on your roof
description=2. Place a rain barrel directly below it (same tile X, Y)
description=3. Repeat #2 as many times as you wish
description=4. Place a sink directly below the last barrel
description=5. Plumb them all together using a pipe wrench
description=
description=The plumbing order doesn't matter, but bottom -> up (sink to roof) will be the most reliable.
description=
description=If you need to reset the "plumbed" status of an object, pick it up and place it down. After placing it, you'll have the option to plumb it again.
description=
description=[h2]Future Plans[/h2]
description=This mod works exactly how I wish it did in the vanilla game. However, the plumbing menu is still dog cheeks. It's very unclear when you can and can't plumb something, and why you can't get water from a sink.
description=

description=
description=[h2]How does it work?[/h2]
description=Plumbing in Project Zomboid is extremely complex. Notably, every time you request water from something, it checks if its external water source is plumbed to something else. If it is, it rejects it as a valid source. This mod (basically) bypasses those checks, letting you plumb any number of rain barrels together.
description=
description=The actual details are more complex than that, so I urge you to read the source code. You can find it here: https://github.com/kmcgurty/MultiFloorPlumbing
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=Works in singleplayer and multiplayer b42. Tested on build 42.21.0.
description=
description=Tested with all 4 rain barrel types, a washer machine, and 2 types of sinks.
description=
description=If any of the code around plumbing changes, this mod will very likely break. It's extremely specific to the game's current logic, and takes advantage of a couple Java functions. It is probably unwise to combine multiple plumbing mods.
